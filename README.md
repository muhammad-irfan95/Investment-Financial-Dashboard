# 📊 Investment & Financial Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an interactive **Investment & Financial Analysis Dashboard** built in **Microsoft Power BI**, backed by **MySQL** and **Python** for data cleaning and preparation.

The dashboard provides clear visibility into capital inflows, profit generation, monthly investor payouts, fund-type performance, payment mode distribution, and top-performing participants and partners. Its main goal is to convert raw financial records into actionable executive insights.

---

* **MySQL** (Data Querying, Aggregation & Structuring)

## 📌 Dashboard Features

1. **Total Capital by Year and Month (Trend)**
   * Tracks monthly growth and compares Invested Capital against Profit Generated over time.
2. **Total Capital by Fund Type**
   * Evaluates capital distribution across fund categories (*Assured Commission Pro*, *Assured Commission Lite*, *Custom Fund*, etc.).
3. **Total Capital by Payment Mode**
   * Breaks down total investments across payment channels (*NEFT/IMPS*, *Cheque*, *Cash*, and *Other*).
4. **Top 5 Participants by Capital**
   * Highlights top investors along with their Participant ID, Bank details, and Invested Capital.
5. **Top 5 Partners by Total Commission**
   * Showcases top commission-earning partners, Partner IDs, Bank details, and Commission amounts.
6. **Interactive Slicers & Filters**
   * Enables dynamic report filtering by **Investment Date**, **Fund Type**, **Deposit Branch**, and **Status**.

* Monitor overall capital inflow, profit margins, and monthly investor payouts accurately.
* Identify top-performing fund plans and preferred payment modes.

## 💡 Key Insights

* **Total Profit Generated:** ₹75 Cr 16 L 65 K with an overall **10% ROI**.
* **Total Payout:** ₹4 Cr 59 L 85 K disbursed.
* **Fund Performance:** *Assured Commission Pro* and *Assured Commission Lite* drive the majority of invested capital.
* **Payment Distribution:** NEFT/IMPS leads payment modes (~64.88%), followed by Cheque (~32.2%).

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Download or clone this repository to your local system.
2. Open the `.pbix` file in **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use the top slicers (**Investment Date**, **Fund Type**, **Deposit Branch**, **Status**) to filter the dashboard.
4. Hover over charts and tables for detailed tooltips and cross-filtering.
